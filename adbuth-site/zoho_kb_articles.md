### Article 2: How to Order Digital Templates (Shop Guide)
* **Category:** Help & Shop Guide
* **Folder:** Ordering & Purchases
* **Associated Page:** [Shop Hub](https://adbuthverse.com/shop)

#### Content:
Adbuth Verse offers a curated selection of productized creative assets, premium digital templates, and "Save the Date" e-invitations. 

**Steps to Place an Order:**
1. **Browse the Shop:** Visit the shop page at https://adbuthverse.com/shop and filter templates by category (e.g., Wedding, Birthday, Corporate Greetings).

5. **Secure Payment:** Pay securely using our checkout gateways. Once payment is successful, you will receive an instant email confirmation.

### Article 3: Template Customization and Edit Requests (How Customization Works)
* **Category:** Help & Shop Guide
* **Folder:** Customization
* **Associated Page:** [Shop Details](https://adbuthverse.com/shop)

#### Content:
All templates purchased from the Adbuth Verse Shop can be customized to match your specific event, branding, or personal details.

**How Customization Works:**
1. **Submit Details:** After purchasing a template, you can submit your custom details (names, dates, location, music choices, and photos/videos) during checkout or through your order dashboard.
2. **Creative Editing:** Our professional design team applies your custom inputs directly to the template. We verify text alignment, color consistency, and audio synchronization.
3. **Review Phase:** We send you a preview draft link. Each template purchase includes up to two rounds of revisions.
4. **Final Approval:** Once you approve the draft, we render the final high-definition version and prepare it for delivery.

### Article 4: Digital Delivery and Downloading your Links (How Delivery Works)
* **Category:** Help & Shop Guide
* **Folder:** Shipping & Delivery
* **Associated Page:** [Customer Orders Dashboard](https://adbuthverse.com/orders)

#### Content:
Adbuth Verse is a fully digital-first creative platform. All design templates, customized videos, and graphic assets are delivered digitally.

**How Digital Delivery Works:**
1. **Email Notification:** As soon as our editors complete rendering your customized file, you will receive an email containing a secure download link (hosted on cloud storage or Google Drive).
2. **Dashboard Downloads:** Registered users can log in to their account and visit their dashboard at https://adbuthverse.com/orders to see all previous and active orders. Click on the order to download files directly.
3. **Link Access:** Download links are active for 30 days after delivery. We recommend downloading and backing up your final files immediately.
4. **Delivery Timeframe:** Pre-made template downloads are available instantly. Customized templates take 24 to 48 hours to process and deliver.

### Article 19: Refund and Service Cancellation Policy
* **Category:** Policies
* **Folder:** Policy Documents
* **Associated Page:** [Refund Policy](https://adbuthverse.com/refund)

#### Content:
Our **Refund and Service Cancellation Policy** governs financial settlements, order changes, and revisions.

**Summary of Terms:**
* **Pre-made Digital Templates:** Once a raw pre-made template download link is accessed or shared, the order is non-refundable due to the nature of digital goods.
* **Customization Services:** If our creative team has already started customizing a template, a partial refund may be issued minus processing and labor costs. No refunds are available once the final draft is approved and delivered.
* **Custom Video Editing Projects:** Deposit payments are required to begin project work. Refund amounts for cancelled projects are calculated based on work completed.
* **Revisions:** If you are unhappy with a video edit, we provide up to 2 rounds of revisions for template orders, or standard contract revisions for custom work.

Read the full Refund Policy here: https://adbuthverse.com/refund
